Plug a webcam, endoscope, microscope or HDMI capture adapter into your phone's USB port and HardLine shows the picture. It records, takes stills, and serves the camera to your network.

HardLine talks to the camera itself, so it does not need root and does not depend on the phone's own support for external cameras. Android 8.0 or newer, with a USB port that works in host mode.

<b>Cameras</b>
• Any USB Video Class camera: YUY2, UYVY, NV12, MJPEG, H.264 and H.265
• Brightness, contrast, exposure, focus, zoom and the camera's other controls, remembered per camera
• USB audio, mixed with the phone's microphone if you want
• The button on the camera takes a picture or starts a recording

<b>Recording</b>
• MP4 with H.264, HEVC or AV1 and AAC sound
• Split files, loop recording within a storage budget
• Motion detection with e-mail alerts and FTP upload

<b>Streaming</b>
• A web page to watch and control the camera from a browser
• RTSP server for VLC, OBS and video recorders
• Live push to RTMP and SRT servers
• Optional HTTPS

<b>On screen</b>
• Time, battery, position or your own text over the picture, and a watermark
• The phone's camera or screen as an inset
• Picture-in-picture, a floating window and a lock-screen view

No ads, no analytics, no account. Free software under the GNU GPL 3.0.
